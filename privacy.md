    <p>At Difficult Calls Law Firm, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients' personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our services.</p>

    <p>We may collect the following types of information:</p>
    <ul>

        <li>Financial information for payment processing</li>

    <p>We use the collected information for:</p>
    <ul>

        <li>Maintaining compliance with legal requirements</li>
        <li>Improving our service offerings</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We may share information with:</p>

        <li>Co-counsel or legal partners when necessary for client representation</li>
        <li>Legal authorities when required by law</li>

        <li>Third parties with your explicit consent</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encryption, secure server infrastructure, and regular security audits.</p>

    <h2>Client Confidentiality</h2>
